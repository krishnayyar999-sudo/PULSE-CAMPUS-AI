## 📌 Problem Statement
Campus infrastructure breakdowns, safety hazards, and student grievances often face long delays due to manual ticketing. High-risk safety threats (like electrical sparking or water leaks) require immediate priority triage.

## 💡 Solution
PulseCampus AI is an automated triage and dispatch portal powered by Zoho Catalyst Intelligent Services:
- **Instant Sentiment & Severity Scoring:** Evaluates grievance urgency (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`) in real time.
- **Smart Department Routing:** Automatically extracts context keywords and assigns tickets to the responsible campus department (Electrical Safety, Civil Maintenance, IT Ops, etc.).
- **Live Metrics Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into reported incidents and triage statuses.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack
- **Cloud Platform:** Zoho Catalyst
- **Hosting:** Catalyst Web Client Hosting
- **AI / Triage Engine:** Catalyst Zia Services (Text Intelligence & Sentiment Analysis)
- **Frontend:** Modern Responsive Single-Page Architecture (Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons)
